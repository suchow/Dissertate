# Guidance for working with this dissertation

This template is for a Duke Graduate School Ph.D. dissertation. It does not implement the special placement of illustrations required for Art History dissertations or the wording for master's theses. Follow the current [Graduate School instructions](https://gradschool.duke.edu/academics/theses-and-dissertations/) and the [ETD guide](https://gradschool.duke.edu/sites/default/files/2022-03/ETD%20Guide%202020.pdf). The PDF filename contains 2020, but the document identifies its revision as October 2025. Its sample pages are references; the guide warns that they may not satisfy every formatting requirement. Apply the written requirements when a sample differs.

Use US Letter paper, a 1.5-inch left margin, and 1-inch top, right, and bottom margins. The bottom margin includes the space below the page number. The template leaves extra space below the body text for the bottom-centered page number. All text, illustrations, tables, footnotes, and page numbers must remain inside these margins. Ordinary text, lists, and the abstract are double-spaced. The guide permits single spacing within long contents and bibliography entries, long headings, indented quotations, footnotes, and captions. Bibliography entries are separated by double spacing. Use one bibliography after the appendices. Footnotes use Arabic numbers starting at 1 in each chapter.

In `frontmatter/personalize.tex`, set the author and Title Case title. Set `\department` to the department or program name without the prefix “Department of”. Set `\dukedefensedate{Month day, year}` and `\dukesubmissionyear{year}` to the defense date and submission year. The submission year defaults to `\degreeyear`. Replace the committee placeholders with the professional names recorded in DukeHub, without Dr., Ph.D., or other professional titles. Set the entire approval block with `\dukecommittee{...}`; separate its lines with `\\[12pt]`. Identify the dissertation advisor explicitly if the committee chair and advisor differ. Confirm the committee names with the departmental graduate studies assistant.

The title page, abstract title page, and copyright page have no printed numbers. The abstract begins at iv, and subsequent preliminary pages continue in lowercase Roman numerals. The Introduction starts at Arabic 1. The default preliminary sequence includes a dedication, contents, lists of tables and figures, and acknowledgements. Remove an unused dedication, acknowledgement section, or illustration list from the `\frontmatter` definition in `Dissertate.cls`. Add any required list of schemes, abbreviations, or supplemental files in the order specified by the guide. Keep the dedication out of the contents; include the abstract and other required sections. Do not leave blank sections or blank pages in the submission. The optional biography belongs after the bibliography, ordinarily occupies no more than one page, and uses third-person prose without birth details or a CV.

The template disables `hyperref` link generation and bookmarks. Confirm that the final PDF contains no hyperlinks, internal link annotations, or bookmarks, including those generated by citations, contents entries, and web addresses. Website references should appear in the same black font as the body, without underlining. Garamond is one of the guide's typical accepted fonts; the template uses EB Garamond. Verify all embedded fonts and any fonts introduced by added packages. Use bold and italics only in the circumstances listed by the guide. For landscape pages, implement and inspect the guide's rotated margins and page-number placement separately.

Edit `frontmatter/abstract.tex` for the abstract and chapter files for the dissertation text. The first body chapter must be titled “Introduction”. Place table titles and captions above tables, and figure titles and captions below figures. Use short titles in the lists of tables and figures. The template uses XeLaTeX and BibTeX. The guide recommends current Adobe Acrobat for PDF conversion and warns that an alternate PDF creator may produce an invalid file; a successful XeLaTeX build does not establish acceptance by Duke. Preserve the editable source and follow the current submission review process.

Write in English. A foreign-language research dissertation requires the approvals specified by the guide, requested at the prospectus stage. Even with approval, the title, abstract, copyright notice, committee pages, and contents remain in English.

Consult Duke's [brand guidelines](https://brand.duke.edu/logos/) before adding institutional branding. Add a seal or logo only when the author requests it and the applicable guidelines allow it. The dissertation guide also prohibits images unrelated to the research.
