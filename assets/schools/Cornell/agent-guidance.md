# Guidance for working with this dissertation

This template is for a Cornell Graduate School Ph.D. dissertation. Consult the current [formatting requirements](https://gradschool.cornell.edu/academic-progress/degrees-fields/research-degree-requirements/thesis-dissertation/writing-your-thesis-dissertation/formatting-requirements/) and [section guidance](https://gradschool.cornell.edu/academic-progress/degrees-fields/research-degree-requirements/thesis-dissertation/writing-your-thesis-dissertation/required-sections-guidelines-and-suggestions/) before changing the layout. Cornell distinguishes mandatory formatting from suggested preliminary-page layouts. Verify the applicable rules separately for Weill Cornell and other degree programs.

Use US Letter paper, margins of at least 1 inch, an embedded main-text font of at least 11 points, and double-spaced body text. Keep page numbers at least 0.75 inch from the paper edge. Preliminary pages use the suggested numbering: the title and copyright pages are counted without printed numbers, the abstract pages are excluded from the count, and the biographical sketch begins at iii. The body starts at 1. Write the dissertation in English. Use the degree-conferral month and year; Cornell lists May, August, and December as conferral months.

Edit `frontmatter/personalize.tex` for the author's details and `resources/biography.tex` for the biographical sketch. The main document is `dissertation.tex`; chapters are in `chapters/`. Preserve the author's text and verify changes in the compiled PDF. The template uses XeLaTeX and BibTeX.

Consult Cornell's [names and marks policy](https://policy.cornell.edu/policy-library/use-cornells-name-logos-trademarks-and-insignias) before adding institutional branding. Include an institutional logo or seal only when the applicable dissertation formatting rules require or recommend it. Use the specified mark and placement. When those rules are silent, omit institutional marks.

## House design and school overrides

Cornell’s [section guidance](https://gradschool.cornell.edu/academic-progress/degrees-fields/research-degree-requirements/thesis-dissertation/writing-your-thesis-dissertation/required-sections-guidelines-and-suggestions/) explicitly distinguishes suggested layouts from submission requirements. The title, copyright, abstract, biographical sketch, acknowledgments, and contents remain included. Suggested page counting is retained for consistency. The title retains its degree, university, author, and conferral metadata. Its large colored title, small caps, and flexible vertical composition restore the house design. The abstract retains its school metadata with a large colored title; preliminary headings use house small caps.

The [formatting requirements](https://gradschool.cornell.edu/academic-progress/degrees-fields/research-degree-requirements/thesis-dissertation/writing-your-thesis-dissertation/formatting-requirements/) require embedded main text of at least 11 points and double spacing, with exceptions for captions, lists, quotations, notes, and bibliography entries. Keep 12-point EB Garamond and the base Lato captions. Black text is recommended; limited color is allowed. Cornell red `B31B1B` is documented in the university’s [brand resources](https://human.cornell.edu/about/communications/brand-resources). Use it for limited title, chapter, and link accents. Retain the original large chapter numeral, chapter-start spacing, epigraph composition, and contents fonts and spacing.

The centered 5.75-inch text block has 1.375-inch lateral margins. The earlier 1.6-inch left and 1.1-inch right dimensions describe Cornell’s sample templates; they are not mandatory dimensions. The one-inch top margin, 1.1-inch bottom text margin, 0.25-inch footer skip, and centered page numbers preserve clearance. Check decoration and page-number glyphs in the rendered PDF against the one-inch material boundary and 0.75-inch page-number boundary. The optional illustrated colophon follows the bibliography, as in the house style.

The template retains English prose and the school’s suggested conferral-month metadata. Review the next rendering for title wrapping, epigraph placement, margins, captions, contents, and embedded fonts. Source inspection alone does not establish acceptance.

Citation and URL text remains black within running text. School color is confined to permitted title and chapter accents.

The margin requirement recommends at least one inch. It describes 1.6-inch left and 1.1-inch right margins as the settings in its templates. Those settings are not a binding requirement; the centered house measure provides 1.375 inches on each side. The separate section-layout page explicitly supplies suggestions beyond the formatting requirements, so its title example does not require an uppercase title. Required title content remains.
