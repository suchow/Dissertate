# Guidance for working with this dissertation

This template covers Rackham Ph.D. dissertations at the University of Michigan. D.M.A. and A.Mus.D. submissions require different treatment. Follow the current [Rackham formatting rules](https://rackham.umich.edu/navigating-your-degree/formatting-guidelines/) and [Dissertation Handbook](https://rackham.umich.edu/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/dissertation-handbook.pdf). The current HTML and handbook title sample require a 2-inch top margin; an older checklist specifies 2.5 inches. This template follows the current instructions. The handbook at the May 2025 URL identifies its edition as July 2023.

Use title case for the dissertation title. In `frontmatter/personalize.tex`, set `\department` to the official program name without “Department of” or “Program in”; the title page supplies parentheses. Set `\degreeyear` to the degree-conferral year, and verify any Medical Scientist Training Program (MSTP) exception. Use `\michigancommittee{...}` for the full committee, with line breaks between names. Put the chair or co-chairs first; alphabetize co-chairs and other members by surname. Include correct ranks, chair roles, and external affiliations.

Set `\michiganlegalname`, `\michiganemail`, and `\michiganorcid` to the required identifier-page values. Their defaults are explicit placeholders. Replace every identifier-page and committee placeholder before submission. The title page and optional frontispiece are uncounted. The identifier page counts as i without a printed number. The following preliminary page is ii. The abstract is the final preliminary section; the body begins at 1. Remove unused optional sections and lists from the `\frontmatter` definition in `Dissertate.cls`. Add a frontispiece through `\michiganfrontispiece`, a preface through `\michiganpreface`, and additional required lists through `\michiganextralists`; each must end with `\clearpage`.

The template uses US Letter paper, 12-point black body text, double spacing, and embedded fonts. Major sections begin with a 2-inch top margin; other margins are at least 1 inch. Headings must not exceed 16 points. Use a font size of at least 9 points for footnotes, captions, tables, figures, and equations. Lists and bibliography entries are single spaced, with a blank line between bibliography entries. Web links are blue and underlined; internal links remain plain. Check every long URL in the rendered PDF for line breaking and margins.

The template sets PDF title and English-language metadata. Change `pdflang` for another principal language. XeLaTeX output has not been validated for accessibility tagging: verify heading structure, language changes, alt text, tables, and reading order using an accessibility checker before submission. A visually correct PDF alone does not prove compliance.

Compile `dissertation.tex` with XeLaTeX and BibTeX. Inspect page numbering, margins, font embedding, headings, and links in the final PDF. Consult the university [brand guidelines](https://brand.umich.edu/) before adding institutional branding. Include an institutional logo or seal only when the applicable dissertation formatting rules require or recommend it. Use the specified mark and placement. When those rules are silent, omit institutional marks.

The template retains EB Garamond body text, Lato captions, smallcaps section headings and contents entries, and right-aligned chapter headings. Rackham requires the Chapter label and limits headings to 16 points; the chapter number and title therefore use 16 points. Epigraphs follow these compact headings in normal flow. The base oversized-number epigraph composition would require additional vertical space. The two-inch opening margin, minimum ancillary sizes, external-link treatment, section sequence, identifiers, and page counters remain school-specific requirements. Single-spaced lists, captions, and reference entries follow recommendations.

Title and chapter accents use Michigan Blue `#00274C` ([university library palette](https://design-system.lib.umich.edu/visual-elements/colors/)). This choice interprets Rackham's black-text requirement as applying to body text, as written. Heading color is discretionary; it is not expressly approved by the dissertation rules. Ordinary body text stays black. Confirm any stricter departmental style requirements before submission.

The body retains the house text width of 5.75 inches, centered horizontally on US Letter paper. The wider side margins preserve the school constraints described above.

The optional colophon follows the references and appendices. No inspected rule explicitly requires the bibliography to be the final page in this edition.
