# Guidance for working with this dissertation

This template covers UCLA Doctor of Philosophy dissertations. Its authority is the [Electronic Thesis and Dissertation Manual](https://ucla.box.com/v/etd-filing-requirements), updated April 2026, including the doctoral samples in Appendix A. The older 42-page manual is superseded. Verify current filing instructions through [UCLA Graduate Education](https://grad.ucla.edu/academics/graduate-study/thesis-and-dissertation-filing-requirements/). Master's theses and other doctoral degrees require separate layouts.

Use US Letter paper, embedded 12-point EB Garamond body text, and margins of at least 1 inch for all manuscript content except page numbers. UCLA lists Garamond at 12 points among its recommended fonts. The template uses a 1.1-inch bottom text margin to leave space for footnotes and bottom-centered page numbers. Keep page numbers at least 0.75 inch from the bottom edge. Check every embedded font in the final PDF, including fonts in figures, captions, and mathematics.

Double-space the manuscript, including preliminary sections, except for the manual's permitted single-spaced footnotes, bibliography entries, long quotations, captions, and items in lists and tables. Single-spaced footnotes and bibliography entries need a blank line between entries. Check footnote spacing in the final PDF. The bibliography is the last section; appendices precede it. This template uses one final bibliography. A chapter-bibliography arrangement requires separate implementation and table-of-contents entries.

The title page counts as i without a printed number. The next page is a required copyright page or blank page, neither counted nor numbered. The abstract starts at ii. The committee page follows the abstract with the next Roman number, which depends on the abstract's length. Dedication, contents, applicable lists, acknowledgments or preface, and the required vita continue Roman numbering. The body starts at Arabic 1 and continues through the appendices and bibliography.

In `frontmatter/personalize.tex`, set the title and official registered author name. Set `\department` to the exact approved major and `\degreeyear` to the degree-award year. Use the degree-award year consistently on all preliminary pages; it may differ from the filing year. Spell out title acronyms, abbreviations, and formulae as the manual requires. Use only permitted special characters. The title page, abstract, and committee page must agree with university records.

Set `\uclachair{[CHAIR FULL NAME]}` and `\uclacommittee{[MEMBER ONE]\\[MEMBER TWO]}` in the personalization file. The same chair name appears automatically on the abstract and committee page. List every committee member without titles or degrees, and list chairs last. The abstract automatically uses “Professor” and “Chair”; the committee page uses “Committee Chair” without “Professor.” For co-chairs, redefine `\uclaabstractchairs` with one `Professor [FULL NAME], Co-Chair\par` line per chair and `\uclacommitteechairs` with matching `[FULL NAME], Committee Co-Chair\par` lines. Replace every placeholder and verify all names against the electronic approval record.

The copyright notice is included by default. To retain the required page but leave it blank, use `\togglefalse{UCLACopyrightNotice}`. To omit the optional dedication, use `\renewcommand{\ucladedication}{}`. Acknowledgments are required for permission-dependent reproduced material, co-authored material, or funding support; otherwise, they are optional. If none of those conditions applies, omit them with `\renewcommand{\uclaacknowledgments}{}`. Add a preface through that command if appropriate. Include applicable lists of figures, tables, symbols, acronyms, supplementary materials, and a glossary after the contents; use `\uclaextralists` for additional lists. Remove unused figure or table lists from the `\frontmatter` definition in `Dissertate.cls`.

Edit the delivered `resources/biography.tex` for the required vita. Limit it to two pages. Include previous education, academic or professional employment, publications, presentations, and other relevant scholarly accomplishments. Exclude the degree for which this dissertation is written and personal information such as birth details, address, or marital status. UCLA recommends a 350-word abstract for the online repository; the abstract in the manuscript PDF has no word limit.

The template uses XeLaTeX and BibTeX for visual formatting. Its output is untagged and has not been validated for accessibility. UCLA requires WCAG 2.1 Level AA conformance before submission as of April 2026. Complete a separate tagging and remediation workflow, then assess the final PDF with an accessibility checker and manual checks for reading order, headings, lists, table headers, image alternative text, contrast, and metadata. Visual inspection and successful compilation do not demonstrate accessibility conformance. The author must certify the manuscript's accessibility during filing. No accessibility validator or remediation tool is installed by this template.

Consult the official [UCLA brand guidelines](https://brand.ucla.edu/) before adding institutional marks. Add a seal or logo only when the author requests it and the applicable guidelines permit it. No institutional mark is included.
