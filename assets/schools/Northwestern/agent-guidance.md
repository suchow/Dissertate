# Guidance for working with this dissertation

This template covers Northwestern TGS Ph.D. dissertations. Follow the current [formatting requirements](https://www.northwestern.edu/tgs/academic-policies-procedures/dissertation-publication/dissertation-formatting-requirements.html) and [sample pages](https://www.northwestern.edu/tgs/documents/academic-policies-procedures/dissertation-formatting-pdf-template.pdf).

Use US Letter paper and 1-inch margins. Print an Arabic page number in the upper-right corner of every page after the title page, at least 0.75 inch from the top and right edges. The title page counts as page 1 without a printed number; the next page is 2. Continue numbering through the body and appendices. Double-space the abstract, acknowledgment, dedication, table of contents, and body. Single spacing is permitted for quotations, captions, lists, tables, notes, and bibliography entries.

In `frontmatter/personalize.tex`, set the author, mixed-case title, and degree-conferral month and year. Set `\department` to the official academic program. The optional copyright page is enabled; disable it with `\togglefalse{NorthwesternCopyright}`. Keep one final bibliography before appendices. Foreign-language dissertations require an English abstract and a 10–20-page English summary appendix.

Compile with XeLaTeX and BibTeX. Preserve the author's text and inspect pagination, font embedding, and spacing in the PDF. Add optional preface, abbreviations, glossary, and illustration lists in the official order when needed.

Consult Northwestern’s [visual identity guidelines](https://www.northwestern.edu/brand/visual-identity/logos-lockups/) before adding institutional branding. Include an institutional logo or seal only when the applicable dissertation formatting rules require or recommend it. Use the specified mark and placement. When those rules are silent, omit institutional marks.

Design choices: the large chapter numeral, chapter-title composition, epigraph placement, contents spacing, and caption fonts follow the shared Dissertate design. The [formatting requirements](https://www.northwestern.edu/tgs/academic-policies-procedures/dissertation-publication/dissertation-formatting-requirements.html) permit legible, non-script, non-ornamental fonts and refer unlisted style questions to the adviser, committee, and program style manual. They do not specify a compact chapter numeral or prohibit color. The template uses [Northwestern Purple, #4E2A84](https://www.northwestern.edu/brand/visual-identity/color-palettes/), for chapter numerals and citation and URL accents. This accent is a house design choice. The margin rule specifies 1 inch on all sides, with listed exceptions; it does not describe minimums. The template retains that specified width. Retain the required title-page layout, margins, spacing, and continuous Arabic pagination. Confirm any additional program requirements before filing.

The optional colophon follows the references and appendices. No inspected rule explicitly requires the bibliography to be the final page in this edition.
