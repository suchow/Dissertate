# Guidance for working with this dissertation

This template covers a Stanford departmental or program Ph.D. dissertation. It excludes the Graduate School of Business, Graduate School of Education, School of Law, Engineer theses, and D.M.A. submissions. Follow the current [format requirements](https://studentservices.stanford.edu/my-academics/earn-my-degree/graduate-degree-progress/dissertations-and-theses/prepare-your-work-0) and [departmental Ph.D. title-page example](https://studentservices.stanford.edu/my-academics/earn-my-degree/graduate-degree-progress/dissertations-and-theses/prepare-your-work-1). Current written instructions take precedence over older examples.

Use US Letter paper, a 1.5-inch left margin, and 1-inch margins on the other sides. This single-sided layout uses a 1.1-inch bottom margin to accommodate text descenders. The body is double-spaced, with single spacing for conventional exceptions such as quotations, captions, and bibliography entries. Page numbers are centered at the bottom, at least 0.5 inch from the bottom edge of the paper. A double-sided manuscript requires a separate review of alternating inner margins and blank-page numbering.

The title page uses uppercase text, regular weight, and no printed page number. In `frontmatter/personalize.tex`, set the title and author. Set `\department` to the official department or program name. The submission statement defaults to “Department of”; for a program, add `\stanfordunitprefix{PROGRAM IN}` and use only the program name in `\department`. Set `\degreemonth` and `\degreeyear` to the month and year of electronic submission, which may differ from degree conferral. Replace formulas and special symbols in the title with words. Verify the submission statement against the applicable departmental or program sample.

The title page counts as i. The upload PDF physically omits copyright page ii and signature page iii; Axess inserts them in the stored dissertation. The abstract is the second physical page and displays iv. Subsequent preliminary pages continue the Roman sequence, and the body begins at Arabic 1. Do not insert blank placeholders for the two system pages. The online abstract has a separate 5,000-character limit and must use plain text. See the [submission system instructions](https://studentservices.stanford.edu/my-academics/earn-my-degree/graduate-degree-progress/dissertations-and-theses/how-use-dissertation).

Design choices: the template uses the shared Dissertate fonts: 12-point EB Garamond body text, Lato captions, and Source Code Pro code. The [format requirements](https://studentservices.stanford.edu/my-academics/earn-my-degree/graduate-degree-progress/dissertations-and-theses/prepare-your-work-0) require standard, non-script, non-ornamental fonts and black type. Their font examples begin with “include”; this template interprets that list as nonexclusive and uses ordinary open-source Garamond. Stanford does not name Garamond in the examples. Confirm the choice against department or adviser requirements; this interpretation is not university approval. Keep mathematical fonts embedded. Compile with XeLaTeX and BibTeX, and inspect all embedded fonts in the final PDF.

The large chapter numeral, chapter-title composition, epigraph placement, and contents spacing follow the shared house design. The written rules require suitable, consistent body headings but specify no chapter-numeral size. The exact title-page format, school margins, spacing, pagination, and black type remain school requirements. Smaller caption type is permitted. The margin rule specifies 1.5 inches on the inner edge and 1 inch elsewhere; it does not describe minimums. The template retains that school geometry, including its bottom text allowance.

The main document is `dissertation.tex`; chapters are in `chapters/`. Preserve the author’s text and inspect the compiled PDF after layout changes. English is required unless the school dean approves an exception. An approved foreign-language dissertation requires an extended English summary, usually 15–20 pages, submitted as a supplemental file. Verify the current submission requirements for that case.

Consult Stanford’s [identity guidance](https://identity.stanford.edu/) before adding institutional branding. Include an institutional logo or seal only when the applicable dissertation formatting rules require or recommend it. Use the specified mark and placement. When those rules are silent, omit institutional marks.

The optional colophon follows the references and appendices. No inspected rule explicitly requires the bibliography to be the final page in this edition.
