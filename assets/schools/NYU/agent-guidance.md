# Guidance for working with this NYU GSAS dissertation

Use the official [NYU GSAS doctoral dissertation formatting requirements](https://gsas.nyu.edu/content/dam/nyu-as/gsas/documents/dissertationsubmissionrelated/Doctoral%20Dissertation%20Formatting%20Requirements%2010-09-15.pdf) and the student's department instructions. The document is dated October 9, 2015. The current GSAS submission webpage was unavailable during the audit; confirm currency with GSAS before submission. Other NYU schools have separate rules.

Retain the house Garamond typography, purple #57068C accents, large chapter numbers, small caps, and epigraph composition. The inspected requirements specify minimum font sizes and spacing without prescribing a compact chapter design or banning color. Use at least ten-point text throughout, including captions and notes. Set one-inch left, right, and top margins. The larger bottom allowance reserves space for the footer and the required two blank lines above it. The requirements give conflicting 0.75-inch and one-inch bottom-folio instructions; this template uses a conservative one-inch footer inset.

Double-space body text and lists, including contents and figure/table lists. Individual bibliography entries must be at least 1.5 spaced. Leave at least two blank lines between headings and body text and between body text or notes and the page number. Verify the actual clearances in the PDF.

The title must be double-spaced and include a solid black adviser signature line. Preserve the school presentation wording and graduation date. The house title remains purple. The official text refers to a separate title-page example; its exact visual spacing was unavailable during this audit. Compare that sample before claiming title-page compliance. Allow additional space for a long title or adviser name.

Count title and copyright pages without printing their numbers. Subsequent preliminary pages use lowercase Roman numerals; their counters follow the pages actually included. The dedication has a heading. A logo frontispiece is omitted under project policy because no mark is required or recommended by the inspected dissertation rules. An optional illustration frontispiece is permitted by those rules, subject to separate rights and identity checks.

Figure and table lists are generated when corresponding float environments occur, including an optional list for a single item. Their auxiliary counts require another LaTeX pass. Material inserted with custom nonfloat commands needs an explicit list check. More than one appendix also requires a list of appendices; add it when expanding the supplied single-appendix example. List entries and references must appear in the contents. Place the optional colophon before references; references must be the final section and final contents entry. Follow the selected disciplinary reference style.

Edit `frontmatter/personalize.tex` for author metadata. Preserve the author's text, and check the compiled PDF after changes. The template uses XeLaTeX and BibTeX. Check [NYU identity resources](https://guides.nyu.edu/posters/logos-images) before changing institutional marks.
