# Working with the Toronto template

This template covers University of Toronto Engineering Science ESC499 undergraduate theses. Graduate dissertations are outside its scope. Before submission, check the current [ESC499 Thesis Format and Requirements](https://engsci.utoronto.ca/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Thesis-Format-Reqs-2026-27.pdf) and the course’s Quercus instructions. The checklist may change during the academic year.

Edit `frontmatter/personalize.tex` for the title, author, supervisor, and submission month and year. Compile `dissertation.tex` with XeLaTeX and BibTeX. The guide names Times New Roman or a comparable font. The template uses 12-point EB Garamond. Body text uses 1.5 line spacing on US Letter paper, with a 1.25-inch left margin and one-inch margins on the other sides. Abstract and acknowledgements are single-spaced. Limit the abstract to 250 words.

The electronic cover is unnumbered; the abstract starts at i. Preliminary sections use centered Roman numbers; the body starts at 1. References precede lettered appendices, and a blank page ends the document. The template uses IEEE references; confirm the discipline’s preferred style with the supervisor.

Printed copies require additional flyleaves and a title page. These are outside this electronic-submission layout.

The course guide requires the Division’s cover template. The bundled `resources/cover.png` reproduces the cover artwork embedded in the guide’s sample; check Quercus for a newer version before submission.

Include an institutional logo or seal only when the applicable dissertation formatting rules require or recommend it. Use the specified mark and placement. When those rules are silent, omit institutional marks.
