# Guidance for working with this Princeton dissertation

Follow the current [Princeton dissertation submission guide](https://libguides.princeton.edu/dissertations_masters_theses_submission/formatting_requirements), its [formatting requirements v26.1, updated April 8, 2026](https://libguides.princeton.edu/ld.php?content_id=77371930), and the student's department instructions. The included older guidelines are historical references.

The 2026 guide recommends black font on the title page, front matter, and body. This template follows that recommendation for chapter numerals, title text, links, and the colophon drop cap. Preserve color in illustrations; the font recommendation does not require grayscale artwork. Keep the house Garamond family, large chapter composition, small caps, and epigraphs. No inspected rule requires compact headings. Retain the 5.75-inch measure and ragged-right body within the one-inch margin guidance.

Double-space the main body. Front matter, back matter, captions, and footnotes may use single or 1.5 spacing. Captions and bibliography text use body-equivalent size; footnotes may be only one point smaller. All fonts must be embedded. The guide accepts other embeddable Type 1 or TrueType fonts alongside its preferred standard families. Verify the actual embedded font representation in the PDF, including bundled OpenType source fonts. Check widow/orphan suppression and heading placement visually.

The first three pages are the unnumbered title, unnumbered copyright notice, and abstract. Start printed Arabic pagination at 3 on the abstract, then continue Arabic numbering through every subsequent page. Keep the abstract within 350 words. Include the abstract in the contents. The copyright notice belongs in the top third of its page. Later section order and citation style follow department or adviser requirements; the optional final colophon may retain its illustration and house composition.

Use the [official title-page format](https://libguides.princeton.edu/ld.php?content_id=77372176) for wording, capitalization, spacing, legal author name, adviser, department or program, degree, and conferral month/year. The title uses the sample’s centered groups, uppercase title and presentation block, mixed-case author/adviser, and text-size title. The supplied one-page sample was inspected directly. Rendered correspondence remains to be checked after this change. Use the degree-conferral date for the Board of Trustees meeting. Do not substitute the defense date.

The PDF uses one-inch margin guidance. The 1.5-inch inner margin belongs to the separate bound-copy requirements when dual submission applies. Check that illustration numbering is consecutive, captions remain readable, and the folio stays in a consistent permitted position at least half an inch from the edge.

Edit `frontmatter/personalize.tex` for metadata, and preserve the author's text. Verify the compiled PDF after changes. The template uses XeLaTeX and BibTeX. Include an institutional mark only when dissertation rules require or recommend it; this is project policy. Consult [Princeton visual identity guidance](https://visualidentity.princeton.edu/about/about) before changing a mark.

Princeton captions retain the house Lato face because its guide specifies scale and embedding without a body-family caption requirement. Harvard’s separate body-font caption instruction does not apply to this edition. The 11-point footnote-size command also affects other uses of that size; review equations, quotations, tables, and the colophon in the complete example.
