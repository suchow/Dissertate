# Guidance for working with this Princeton dissertation

Check the [Graduate School's final public oral examination guidance](https://gradschool.princeton.edu/academics/degrees-requirements/preparing-final-public-oral-examination), its linked dissertation formatting requirements, and the student's department instructions before changing the layout. Verify pagination, margins, font embedding, and the requirements for the intended submission. The template may not reflect every current requirement.

Consult [Princeton's visual identity guidance](https://visualidentity.princeton.edu/about/about) before adding or changing an institutional mark.

Edit `frontmatter/personalize.tex` for the author's details. The main document is `dissertation.tex`; chapters are in `chapters/`. Keep the author's text intact when adjusting layout or metadata. Follow current university and department rules where the template differs, and verify changes in the compiled PDF. The template uses XeLaTeX and BibTeX.

Include an institutional logo or seal only when the applicable dissertation formatting rules require or recommend it. Use the specified mark and placement. When those rules are silent, omit institutional marks.
