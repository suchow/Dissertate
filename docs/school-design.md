# School design decisions

Dissertate uses the Harvard/Generic visual language wherever school instructions permit it: Garamond text, a narrow reading measure, right-aligned chapter compositions, oversized chapter numerals, small caps, restrained accents, Lato captions, and an optional illustrated colophon. Required title wording, page order, pagination, and margins take precedence. School colors identify an edition; they do not establish institutional approval.

This audit separates requirements, recommendations, and design choices. A sample governs the properties that the instructions require authors to reproduce. It supplies no rule for unrelated body typography. An inclusive font list supplies examples. Direct recommendations apply to the property named. Ambiguous broad font-size limits are retained provisionally and identified below. Departmental instructions may add constraints.

## Sources and scope

Each school’s `agent-guidance.md` links its governing formatting source and relevant color reference. The audit used those primary sources and the current style definitions. Penn Guide 15 was parsed from the Provost’s DOCX, without executing embedded content. The supplied Chicago Psychology Word model was read as XML. Neither a successful build nor a visual review establishes institutional acceptance, PDF accessibility, or rights in institutional marks.

The house chapter numeral now uses the bundled Garamond bold face. The former Palatino request selected an unavailable shape under XeLaTeX. School-limited compositions retain normal-flow epigraphs to avoid collision. The optional colophon preserves matching illustration and text widths, one credit paragraph, and the stylized XeLaTeX logo. A text-size initial replaces the drop cap where a broad size ceiling is retained.

## Validation

Complete examples must be rebuilt after the design changes and reviewed page by page. At the initial checkpoint, Penn passed locally; MIT’s earlier build predates its final colophon reorder. Remaining final-source builds and visual reviews are pending. Review title and preliminary pages, chapter openings and quotations, ordinary page transitions, equations, code, captions, contents/lists, appendices, references, and colophons. Confirm required margins, font embedding, resolved references, and absence of clipping and collisions. The source-unavailable UCLA decision remains open until the current manual can be inspected.
